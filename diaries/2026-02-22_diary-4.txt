        - Following success of OPCNN dataset (https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/9537782)
        - https://www.mdpi.com/2078-2489/13/12/576 "Relatively simple feature extraction methods such as TF and TF-IDF
        have achieved strong performance in fake news detection. Riedel et al. [24] reported 88.5% accuracy using an
        MLP with TF and TF-IDF features. Ahmed et al. [25] achieved 92% accuracy with a linear SVM and unigram TF-IDF
        features. Bharadwaj [26] found that a Random Forest with bigram features reached 95.66% accuracy. Study [27]
        showed that character n-grams improved performance more than word n-grams. Additionally, [28] demonstrated that
        TF-IDF and CountVectorizer outperformed state-of-the-art Transformer models such as BERT." (ChatGPT summary)
